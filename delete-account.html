Delete your Duskra Online account

App: Duskra Online · Developer: Duskra Online

Option 1: in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Duskra Online on the device you play on.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Read the warning and tap Continue.
  4. Press and hold Hold to delete for 2 seconds.
  5. Settings → Account now shows the date your account will be deleted.

Option 2: by email (without the app)

Send an email to support@duskraonline.com with the subject Delete my Duskra Online account.

We do not keep your email address or name with your account, so we will reply and tell you which information from the app we need to find it. We use your email only to answer this request.

No longer have the app? In Season 0 every account is a guest account, tied to a key the app kept on your device. Deleting the app deleted that key, so nobody can play that pilot again, and the account is deleted automatically after 180 days without play.

What happens next

  • 14-day waiting period. You can keep playing. To cancel, tap Cancel deletion in Settings → Account; playing or signing in again does not cancel it. The game reminds you each time you sign in. This protects you if you change your mind.
  • After the waiting period you are signed out and the account is deleted within one day (at the latest within 30 days).
  • Deleted: the device link, your ships, equipment, cargo, position, settings and sessions.
  • Kept, but no longer linked to you: the in-game economy record (credits, items, experience) under the old internal pilot number, records of support or moderation actions on that number, and a note of the internal account number with when the deletion was requested and completed.
  • Backups: deleted data can stay in encrypted backups for up to 35 days until they expire.

Deletion cannot be undone. Details are in the privacy policy.
