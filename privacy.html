Duskra Online Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · Applies to the Duskra Online game for iPhone and Android, Season 0

In short: You can play without a form, an email address or a real name. We collect only what the game needs to keep your pilot, run a fair shared world and fix crashes. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data. Season 0 has no chat and no purchases.

1. Who we are

Duskra Online is made by Duskra Online ("we", "us"), who is responsible for your data (the data controller). Contact: support@duskraonline.com.

3. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise or approximate location, photos, camera, microphone, advertising ID or payment details. Season 0 has no chat, no messages between players and no in-app purchases. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software from other companies.

Sign in with Apple or Google is not available yet. If we add it, we will update this policy before it is turned on.

4. Legal bases

We use your data to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract), for security, cheat prevention and crash fixing (our legitimate interest in a safe and working game), and where the law requires it (legal obligation).

5. Who can see your data

  • Other players can see your ship and where it is in the shared world.
  • Hosting provider: our servers, database and backups run at Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the EU (Stockholm, eu-north-1) region. AWS processes the data only on our behalf, as our processor.
  • Apple and Google run the app stores. Their own privacy policies apply to what they collect. If you allowed it in your device settings, they may also share crash reports and statistics with us.
  • Authorities only when we are legally required to.

We do not sell or rent your data and we do not share it for advertising.

6. Deleting your account

In the game: Settings → Account → Delete account. Read the warning, tap Continue, then press and hold the button for 2 seconds.

  • Your account is deleted after a 14-day waiting period. Until then you can keep playing, and Settings → Account shows the date and a Cancel deletion button. Only that button cancels the request: playing or signing in again does not. The game reminds you each time you sign in while a deletion is scheduled.
  • When the 14 days are over, you are signed out and your account is deleted within one day (at the latest within 30 days).
  • Deleted: the device link, your ships, equipment, cargo, position, settings and sessions.
  • Kept, but no longer linked to you: the in-game economy record under the old internal pilot number, records of support or moderation actions taken on that pilot number, and a note of the internal account number with when the deletion was requested, cancelled or completed. Nothing in them identifies you once the device link is gone.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in our encrypted database backups for up to 35 days, until those backups expire. If we ever restore a backup, every deletion is applied again before the game starts.

Without the app: see Delete your Duskra Online account.

Guest accounts (in Season 0, every account is a guest account) are deleted automatically after 180 days without play.

7. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email support@duskraonline.com. Because we do not keep your name or email address with your account, we will tell you which information from the app we need to find your account; we never ask for a password. We answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

8. Season 0

Season 0 is a test season. Game progress may be reset when it ends; we will announce this in advance. A reset does not change the rules in this policy.

9. Children

Duskra Online is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you are below the age at which you can consent to data processing in your country, ask a parent or guardian before playing. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete the account.

10. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (TLS 1.3). Backups are encrypted. The device link is kept in a separate part of the database that the game itself cannot read.

11. International transfers

Our servers are in the European Union. If you play from outside the EU, for example from Turkey, your data is transferred to and stored in the EU.

12. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the game before a change that affects how we use your data takes effect.
