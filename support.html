Frequently asked questions

What is Season 0?

Season 0 is the first test season of Duskra Online, open to testers through TestFlight (iPhone) and Google Play testing (Android). Two maps joined by a portal, one ship, combat against NPC ships, loot, a station (sell, buy, storage, repair and equipment), the Gunnery and Repair skills, and a short guide of 5 steps. Progress may be reset when the season ends; we will announce it in advance.

Do I need an account?

No. Tap Play and you are in space as a guest. Your pilot is tied to a key the app keeps on this device.

Can I keep my pilot on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Not yet. Sign in with Apple or Google is not available in Season 0, so a pilot stays on the device where it was created. Deleting the app deletes the key on the device, and the pilot cannot be recovered after that.

Can I replay the guide?

Yes. Skip it any time with Skip tutorial, and start it again with Settings → Restart tutorial.

The game says "Update required".

The servers need a newer version of the app. Tap Update, or update Duskra Online from TestFlight or Google Play.

I cannot connect, or the connection keeps dropping.

The game reconnects by itself and keeps your place. If it never connects, try another network: some Wi-Fi networks (offices, schools, hotels) block the kind of traffic the game uses. Switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data is fine.

Are there purchases, ads or chat?

No. Season 0 has no in-app purchases, no ads and no chat.

I lost items or credits.

Every change to credits and items is recorded on our servers, so we can check what happened. Email us with the approximate time and what you lost.

The app crashed.

The app sends a short crash report the next time it connects (no account details, see the privacy policy). If it keeps happening, email us with your device model and app version. TestFlight testers can also send feedback from the TestFlight app.

How do I delete my account?

In the game: Settings → Account → Delete account, tap Continue, then press and hold for 2 seconds. Your account is deleted after 14 days. Until then you can cancel with Cancel deletion in Settings → Account; playing again does not cancel it. Without the app, see Delete your Duskra Online account.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Email us. See the privacy policy for what we keep.
